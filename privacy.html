Who we are

Felix is an AI service run by RandomSandwiches. RandomSandwiches is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: hello@felixhq.app, RandomSandwiches, Abu Dhabi, UAE.

What we collect and why

  • Sample brief requests. Your name, work email, agency, website, role, sector, agency size, what you use now and company type. We use this to send your brief, answer questions and understand demand. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in responding to your request.
  • The weekly Watchlist. Your email address (and agency, if you give it), only if you sign up or tick the box, and only once you confirm by email. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in every issue.
  • Founding deposits. Payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email, the amount and a payment reference, never your full card details. Lawful basis: to take and, if asked, refund your deposit.
  • Business outreach. We may email people at limited companies and LLPs about Felix, using business contact details the agency publishes on its own website. Lawful basis: legitimate interests. Every email says how we got your details and lets you opt out with one reply. We don't email sole traders or partnerships without consent, and we never use data scraped from LinkedIn.

About the briefs themselves

Felix briefs name people only in their professional roles, and only where a public source such as a company announcement or official notice names them. Each fact links to its source. If you'd like a fact about you corrected or removed, email us and we'll act on it within 5 working days.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run Felix: Google Workspace (email), FormSubmit (form handling), Stripe (payments), Kit (newsletter sending), Netlify (website hosting) and our email outreach tool. Some of these process data outside the UK under standard contractual safeguards. We don't sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Brief requests and outreach records: up to 24 months from last contact. Watchlist subscriptions: until you unsubscribe. Payment records: as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Email hello@felixhq.app. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
