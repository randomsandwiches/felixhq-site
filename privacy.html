Who we are

Felix is run by RandomSandwiches, Abu Dhabi, UAE. RandomSandwiches is the data controller, responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: hello@felixhq.app.

What we collect and why

  • Free brief requests. Your first name, work email, agency website, kind of agency, role, sector, agency size, what you use now and any brands you name as targets. We work out the agency name from the website. We use this to send your brief, answer questions and understand demand. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in responding to your request.
  • The Shortlist by Felix. Your email address (and agency, if you give it), only if you sign up or tick the box, and only once you confirm by email. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in every issue.
  • Founding deposits. Payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email, the amount and a payment reference, never your full card details. Lawful basis: to take and, if asked, refund your deposit.
  • Business outreach. We may email people in business roles (founders, managing directors and new business or growth leads) at limited companies and LLPs about Felix. We take the person's name and role from the agency's own website: its team, about and news pages. We find and check their work email address with Hunter (hunter.io), a service that builds its data from public web pages and verifies each address. We only email work addresses that Hunter verifies, never personal addresses. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, under a written assessment. Every email names the sender, links to this notice and has a one-click opt-out. If you opt out, your address goes on a permanent suppression list so no RandomSandwiches brand emails you again. We don't email sole traders or unincorporated partnerships without consent, and we never use data scraped from LinkedIn.

About the briefs themselves

Felix briefs name people only in their professional roles, and only where a public source such as a company announcement or official notice names them. Each fact links to its source. If you'd like a fact about you corrected or removed, email us and we'll act on it within 5 working days.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run Felix: Google Workspace (email), FormSubmit (form handling), Stripe (payments), Kit (newsletter sending), Netlify (website hosting and form logging) and Hunter (finding and checking work email addresses) and Instantly (sending outreach emails). Some of these process data outside the UK under standard contractual safeguards. We don't sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Outreach contacts who never reply: deleted by 22 July 2027. Opt-outs: kept permanently on our suppression list, so we never email you again. Brief requests, replies and deposits: up to 24 months from last contact. The Shortlist subscriptions: until you unsubscribe. Payment records: as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Email hello@felixhq.app. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
