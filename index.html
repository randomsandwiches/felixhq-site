One sign is noise. Felix watches them stack up.

A single announcement rarely means a review. A new owner, then a rebuilt senior team, then a new CMO, with no agency confirmed in five years: that's a brand getting ready to look.

Felix reads the public record every day, scores each brand, and alerts you when the score moves up, not when the news breaks. By the time a pitch is announced, everyone with a database has seen it.

A way in that isn't another cold email

A new CMO is flooded with agency emails in her first month, and most go unanswered. So Felix doesn't stop at an opener. For every brand it plans the routes most likely to get you a conversation, in the right order.

  • Warm routes. People you already share with them, found from your own clients and case studies.
  • Gatekeepers. Pitch consultants and advisers likely to shape the review.
  • Something on paper. A one-page point of view, written for you to print and post.
  • Email, at the right moment. One of the routes, not the whole plan.
  • Reasons to stay in touch. Felix flags the next good moment for months, not once.
