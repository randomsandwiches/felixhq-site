Questions

Is Felix a person?

No. Felix is an AI service run by RandomSandwiches. Every brief is produced by software and checked by a second automated reviewer before it's sent, with a human spot-check each week.

Where does the information come from?

Public sources: company announcements, stock exchange notices, company registries such as Companies House, public procurement notices, job adverts on company and charity job boards, and the trade press. Every fact links to its source and shows when it was last checked. We don't scrape LinkedIn and we don't use other vendors' data.

Is this a contact list?

No. Felix names decision-makers only where they're named in public announcements, and tells you the routes in. It's there to help you choose where to spend your time, not to fill an email tool.

Who is it for?

Agencies of any size. We're starting with independent and mid-sized agencies in the UK, where the person who wants it can say yes and pay by card.
